M-Score clinical assessment system and training

Our research group has developed the M-score clinical assessment system and its corresponding standardized evaluation process to standardize the assessment of the severity of symptoms and treatment efficacy in patients with non-lactational mastitis(NLM). The system uses several common clinical symptoms and complaints found in patients with NLM as evaluation items, divided into five modules: pain, lump, erythema, ulceration or fistula, and quality of life.

ⅠIntroduction

The M-score is composed of modules such as mass size, erythema, fistulae/ulceration, pain or tenderness, and impact on quality of life, and is used to describe the patient’s current disease severity. The M-active-score focuses specifically on manifestations of inflammatory activity(erythema, fistulae/ulceration, pain).

Recommended context for assessment：Baseline assessment, followups after standard treatment, observation of treatment effecacy, research documentation, rater training.

Ⅱ M-score module definition

1. Mass size

Measure the greatest diameter of the breast lump. During the examination, use four fingers held together to palpate gently, starting with the unaffected side and then the affected side, and examining the normal areas before the affected areas. If there are multiple lumps, add their greatest diameters together.

This applies only to lesions detectable on physical examination; lesions identified solely on imaging are excluded; nodules resulting from normal glandular hyperplasia or fibroadenomas are not counted.

ScoreExamination result
0 No palpable mass
1 Palpable mass ≤ 3 cm
2 Palpable mass > 3 cm

2. Erythema

The examination focuses primarily on visual inspection, taking current symptoms into account. Palpate the skin over the affected area and compare it with the surrounding healthy skin to determine whether there is any redness or warmth.

Vascular filling sign: Gently pressing the centre of the erythema; when the finger is removed, the skin at the site of pressure changes from white to red, indicating active erythema. This must be distinguished from post-recovery hyperpigmentation.

ScoreExamination result
0 No significant erythema
2 Significant erythema

3. Fistula/ulceration

Check the breast for any skin breakdown or fistulae. This is primarily a visual examination, and the patient should be asked about any discharge of pus and the healing process since the last assessment.

If no fistula is present at the initial consultation, a ‘fistula’ caused by puncture is not considered to constitute the formation of a fistula.

ScoreExamination result
0 No fistula/ulceration
1 There has been pus discharge over the past week, but the physical examination revealed no signs of this
2 On examination, ulceration and purulent discharge were observed

4. Pain / Tenderness

The Visual Analogue Scale (VAS) was used. A score of 0 indicates no pain, whilst a score of 10 indicates an unbearable, intense pain unlike anything previously experienced.

The M-score-a is based on the patient’s current subjective pain; the M-score-t is based on tenderness detected on physical examination. At each assessment, it is recommended to remind the patient of their previous VAS score, or to use the corresponding area on the opposite breast as a 0-point reference.

ScoreExamination result
0 VAS 0–3
1 VAS 4–6
2 VAS 7–10

5. Quality of life

Ask about the impact of the condition on the patient’s quality of life, including work, daily activities, dressing, sleep, wound care and whether any medical intervention is required.

ScoreExamination result
0 No ima+pact
1 Mild impact
2 Severe impact

Case 1

Figure refference

A 47-year-old woman presented with a six-month history of diagnosed non-lactational mastitis. An ultrasound scan revealed a 4 × 4 cm benign mass; the patient underwent an intralesional injection one week ago. Physical examination: On palpation, the mass measured 6 × 4 cm; the vascular filling sign was negative; a scab was visible at the site of the intra-lesional injection; there had been no ulceration or pus discharge in the past week; the patient rated her pain as 3 and tenderness as 4; she experienced occasional stabbing pain during daily work and activities; frequent wound care was not required; and her quality of life was mildly affected.

Correct answers：

M-score-a = Mass size 2 points + Erythema 0 points + Fistula/ulceration 0 points + Pain (patient self-reported) 0 points + Impact on quality of life 1 point = 3 。

M-score-t = Mass size: 2 points + Erythema: 0 points + Fistula/ulceration: 0 points + Pain (tenderness): 1 point + Impact on quality of life: 1 point = 4 。

Explanation：The injection site within the lesion has formed a scab; there has been no ulceration or pus discharge for nearly a week, indicating a tendency towards healing; the score for fistula/ulceration is 0. An intralesional injection was administered one week ago; there is slight skin bruising, no signs of active inflammation, and negative capillary refill; the score for erythema is 0.

Case 2

Figure refference

A 33-year-old woman presented with a one-year history of diagnosed non-lactational mastitis. An ultrasound scan revealed a 2 × 3 cm benign mass; the patient had not received any treatment in the past six months. Physical examination: on palpation, the mass measured 2 × 2 cm; there was no vascular filling sign; the skin appeared normal; the patient reported a pain score of 0 and tenderness of 0; there was no impact on her daily work or life, and her quality of life was unaffected.

Correct answers：

M-score-a = Mass size 1 point + Erythema 0 points + Fistula/ulceration 0 points + Pain (patient self-reported) 0 points + Impact on quality of life 0 points = 1 。

M-score-t = Mass size 1 point + Erythema 0 points + Fistula/ulceration 0 points + Pain (tenderness) 0 points + Impact on quality of life 0 points = 1 。

Explanation：The patient shows no signs of active inflammation; the M-active-score is 0. According to the proposed definitions, an M-score-t of ≤ 1 is considered to indicate resolution, whilst an M-active-score of 0 is considered to indicate inflammatory remission.

Case 3

Figure refference

A 35-year-old woman presenting with a three-month history of diagnosed non-lactational mastitis. Ultrasound revealed a 5 × 8 cm benign mass. The patient has undergone two weeks of anti-tuberculosis treatment with poor results. Physical examination: Palpation revealed a mass measuring 8 × 6 cm with positive vascular filling. The skin over the mass had ulcerated. The patient rated her pain as 7 on a scale of 1 to 10 and tenderness as 10. She frequently needs to dress the wound in her daily life; at its worst, she finds it difficult to change her clothes and has trouble sleeping. She feels anxious about her condition, and her quality of life is significantly impaired.

Correct answers：

M-score-a = Mass size 2 points + Erythema 2 points + Fistula/ulceration 2 points + Pain (patient self-reported) 2 points + Impact on quality of life 2 points = 10 。

M-score-t = Mass size 2 points + Erythema 2 points + Fistula/ulceration 2 points + Pain (tenderness) 2 points + Impact on quality of life 2 points = 10 。

Explanation：The patient presents with extensive lesions, active erythema, ulceration and pus discharge, severe pain and a marked impairment in quality of life, indicating a serious condition that requires further assessment and prompt intervention.
